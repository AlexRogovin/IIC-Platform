# IIC-Platform: Operating Model

**Administrative Enablement Provider — 11-Step Worker Flow**

---

## Actor Model

| Actor | Jurisdiction | Role |
|-------|-------------|------|
| IRA (Indian Recruiting Agent) | India | MEA-registered; sources and screens workers |
| IIC-Platform | Israel/India | Compliance governance, documentation, coordination |
| Ta'agid | Israel | PIBA-licensed EOR; employs workers, pays wages |
| Worker | India | Construction specialist; zero-fee, zero-debt |
| NSDC International | India/G2G | Government coordination channel |
| PIBA | Israel | Regulator; licenses Ta'agidim, monitors compliance |

---

## 11-Step Corridor Flow

```
Step 1:  Ta'agid submits worker quota request to PIBA
Step 2:  PIBA approves quota; notifies Ministry of Interior
Step 3:  IIC-Platform receives quota confirmation from Ta'agid
Step 4:  IIC-Platform coordinates with IRA to open recruitment
Step 5:  IRA screens and shortlists candidates (NSDC/HKRN/MITRA channels)
Step 6:  IIC-Platform verifies IRA compliance (MEA registration, fee receipts)
Step 7:  Workers sign zero-fee employment contract with Ta'agid
Step 8:  IIC-Platform manages documentation package (visas, bank guarantees, insurance)
Step 9:  Workers depart India; IIC-Platform confirms arrival protocols
Step 10: Ta'agid activates payroll; IIC-Platform monitors wage payment
Step 11: IIC-Platform submits monthly compliance report; flags anomalies
```

---

## Revenue Trigger Points

| Step | IIC Revenue Event |
|------|-----------------|
| Step 8 (documentation complete) | Placement fee $300/worker invoiced |
| Monthly (post-Step 10) | Governance retainer $2,500/month billed |
| Worker replacement | New placement fee $300/worker |

---

## IIC-Platform Legal Position

IIC-Platform is explicitly **NOT**:
- The employer (Ta'agid holds all employment contracts)
- A PIBA license holder (Ta'agid holds the license)
- A payroll operator (Ta'agid pays wages directly)
- A recruiter charging workers (zero-fee is absolute)

IIC-Platform **IS**:
- The compliance documentation coordinator
- The IRA due diligence verifier
- The BI monitoring layer (complaint tracking, PIBA reporting)
- The B2B contract counterparty to Ta'agid and IRA

---

## Key Risk Areas

| Risk | Trigger | IIC Response |
|------|---------|-------------|
| IRA charges worker fee | Receipt audit fails | Suspend IRA; notify PIBA |
| Ta'agid underpays worker | Wage monitoring alert | Issue formal notice; escalate |
| Worker complaint filed | PIBA/CIMI notification | Root-cause analysis within 48h |
| IRA loses MEA registration | MEA update | Activate backup IRA immediately |
| PIBA quota change | Ministry announcement | Re-model capacity within 24h |
